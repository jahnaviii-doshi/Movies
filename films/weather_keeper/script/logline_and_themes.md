# The Weather Keeper of Block C — Logline & Themes

## Logline
In a scorching Delhi heatwave, three kids sneak onto the forbidden roof of their housing block and discover that the grumpy old man upstairs secretly makes the city's weather — and he's too old and tired to open this year's monsoon on his own.

## Kid layer
- A secret workshop inside a water tank, with breezes kept in jars, clouds spun from pressure-cooker steam and kites that catch the wind.
- **Bhaap**, a baby cloud who rains when he's sad and sparks tiny lightning when he's happy.
- Rooftop comedy: Rehan's too-fast spin hails out a cricket match, and Captain the pigeon has opinions.
- A real mistake (the dust storm), a lost friend who comes home, and a last-ball bowl of a key into a keyhole.
- The joy of the first monsoon rain.

## Adult layer (never said out loud)
- **Burnout and ageing.** Somebody quietly keeps everything running until they can't. The cardigan in June and the shaking hand say it.
- **Grief.** "It always needed three." Varsha is the missing key, seen only in a photo and a silhouette.
- **Passing on a craft.** You can't force weather, only tend it. The notebook changes hands, and the keeper finally rests.
- **Climate.** Felt only through the heat: the dead fans, Dadi under a still fan, a thermometer that pops. Never preached.

## The poster image
Night, on the roof of Block C. Three kids each turn an iron key in a huge carved trunk, with a tiny glowing cloud pushing the third key with its whole body. Out of the lid, a slate-blue monsoon rolls over the lit skyline of Delhi. One fat raindrop hangs in the air above the trunk, catching the light.
