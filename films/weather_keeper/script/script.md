# THE WEATHER KEEPER OF BLOCK C

*An original animated short film. Runtime 20:00.*

**CAST**
TARA (10). The planner. Round specs, a ponytail so tight it looks like it hurts, a notebook with a pencil tucked in its spiral, a striped T-shirt and a sensible watch.
ISHAAN (7). Tara's little brother. Dreamy, always slightly sticky, a mop of hair, a too-big orange vest, one sock up and one sock down. He talks to pigeons.
REHAN (11). The neighbour. Cricket-mad, gap-kneed, a sweatband on each wrist, a tennis ball always in his fist. Impatient.
MEGHNATH SHARMA, called "KHADOOS UNCLE" (80s). Top floor. Long white eyebrows like awnings, a brown cardigan buttoned up in a heatwave, his right arm in a sling. Grumpy, but never cruel.
BHAAP. A baby cloud about the size of a football. Fluffy, round, two dot eyes, no mouth until he's happy. When he's sad he rains; when he's excited he sparks tiny lightning. Always the softest, whitest thing in frame.
VARSHA. Meghnath's late wife. Seen only in a framed photograph (laughing, hair full of wind) and once as a silhouette.
CAPTAIN. A one-legged-standing pigeon with opinions. Ishaan's best friend before Bhaap.
DADI (O.S. / glimpsed). Tara's grandmother, struggling in the heat. Heard behind a door, and seen once on her bed under a slow fan.

---

## 1. HEATWAVE — 00:00–02:00

**EXT. DELHI SKYLINE — DAY**

The sun is a white coin welded to a white sky. The city wobbles in the heat. A thermometer on a chemist's sign (a red line climbing, no numbers) creeps up and pops its top. *Ping.*

**EXT. BLOCK C, SECTOR 9 — DAY**

A tired 1980s government block: five storeys of peeling cream paint, balconies sagging with laundry, AC units dripping like they're crying. On the roof is a forest of black water tanks and crooked TV antennas. Pigeons pant.

The ceiling fans in a hundred windows turn... and stop. Power cut. A collective, off-screen groan rises from every floor at once: *Uhhhhhh.*

**EXT. BLOCK C COURTYARD — DAY**

A water tanker grinds in. Off-screen, buckets clang and voices squabble.

> **VOICE (O.S.)**
> I was here first!

> **ANOTHER VOICE (O.S.)**
> Since when?

> **VOICE (O.S.)**
> Since breakfast!

**INT. TARA'S FLAT — DAY**

Shutters closed. Stripes of white light. TARA writes in her notebook: a neat list headed with a drawing of a sun, crossed out with a fierce pencil. ISHAAN lies on the cool floor with his cheek on the tiles, talking to CAPTAIN the pigeon on the windowsill.

> **ISHAAN**
> Captain says it's too hot to fly.

> **TARA**
> Pigeons don't say things.

Captain turns his back on her.

Through a half-open door: DADI on her bed under a fan that isn't turning, fanning herself with a newspaper. Slowly. Tara watches, and her pencil stops.

**EXT. STAIRWELL LANDING — DAY**

REHAN bounces a tennis ball off the wall. *Tok. Tok. Tok.* From above, a door creaks. Everyone freezes.

Down the stairs comes MEGHNATH: cardigan buttoned to the throat in the heat, arm in a sling, eyebrows like two white awnings. He stops on the step above Rehan. The ball rolls to his slipper.

He looks at the ball. He looks at Rehan. He does not give the ball back. He climbs back up with it.

> **REHAN** *(whisper)*
> Khadoos Uncle.

> **TARA** *(whisper)*
> He wears a cardigan. In June.

> **ISHAAN** *(whisper)*
> Maybe he's cold inside.

At the top of the stairs is the roof door. It has a fat padlock and a hand-painted sign with a big red cross on it. Meghnath doesn't go through it. He goes into his flat and shuts the door.

---

## 2. THE ROOF — 02:00–04:00

**INT. STAIRWELL — LATE AFTERNOON**

Captain flaps past Ishaan's nose, up the last flight, and slips through a crack where the roof door doesn't quite meet the wall.

> **ISHAAN**
> Captain! You're not allowed!

Ishaan looks down the stairs at nobody. Then he follows, squeezing his small self through the crack with a *pop*.

**EXT. ROOF — CONTINUOUS**

A maze of black water tanks, tangled antenna wire and dried-up flowerpots. The city haze glows orange. Ishaan creeps between the tanks, low and wide-eyed.

A sound: a pressure-cooker **WHISTLE**. *Phweeeeeeet!* (**KEEPER'S THEME**, a lone thin melody)

Ishaan peeks around a tank.

Meghnath sits on an upturned bucket. In front of him is an old pressure cooker on a tiny stove. Its steam rises into a frame of bent TV-antenna wire strung like a loom. With his one good hand, he **spins** the steam the way a grandmother spins wool, around and around, and the steam thickens, whitens and rounds out...

...into a small, perfect, fluffy **cloud**.

The cloud opens two dot eyes. Blinks. Sneezes a tiny spark of lightning. *Tzik.* (**BHAAP'S THEME**, plucked and twinkly)

> **ISHAAN** *(breathless)*
> Whoa.

The cloud, BHAAP, turns. Sees Ishaan. Zooms straight at him and bumps his face like a puppy. It's cool and damp. Ishaan giggles.

> **MEGHNATH**
> WHO is on my roof?

Ishaan freezes. Bhaap hides behind Ishaan's head, which hides nothing.

Behind them the roof door scrapes open. TARA and REHAN tumble through, having followed.

> **TARA**
> Ishaan, we are going to be in so much—

She sees the cloud.

> **TARA**
> —...trouble.

Three kids. One grumpy old man. One cloud. Nobody moves.

---

## 3. RECRUITED — 04:00–07:00

**EXT. ROOF — CONTINUOUS**

Meghnath stands up. Even with the sling and the stoop, he is tall, and his eyebrows come down like shutters.

> **MEGHNATH**
> Out.

> **REHAN**
> You made a cloud.

> **MEGHNATH**
> Out.

> **TARA**
> You MADE a CLOUD.

Meghnath turns back to his loom. He tries to spin a second cloud one-handed. The steam slips through his fingers and tears into wisps. He tries again. His good hand shakes. He winces, and the sling pulls.

Ishaan quietly steps up and holds the other end of the wire.

The steam catches and spins. A wisp becomes a puff.

Meghnath looks down at Ishaan for a long moment.

> **MEGHNATH**
> You touch nothing. You do exactly as I say.

Rehan pumps a fist. Tara opens her notebook.

> **TARA**
> What are the rules?

> **MEGHNATH**
> That was the rules.

**EXT. ROOF / BIG WATER TANK — CONTINUOUS**

He leads them to the oldest, biggest, rustiest tank on the roof. A small iron hatch is bolted into its side. He opens it.

**INT. THE TANK WORKSHOP — CONTINUOUS** (**KEEPER'S THEME**, the melody grows warmer)

Inside, impossibly, it is huge: a dim, cool, silver-blue cathedral. Shelves spiral up its curved walls, crowded with glass jars, and in every jar is a little **breeze**, swirling: a pale-blue morning breeze, a green evening breeze smelling of rain, a dusty yellow loo wind rattling its lid. Kites hang from the ceiling like sleeping bats. Wooden looms hold half-woven clouds. Labelled tins (labels are drawings: a sun, a snowflake, a swirl) are stacked high, and a sealed tin trunk sits in the corner with a padlock and chains.

In the middle, under a dusty cloth, a huge shape sits. Something big.

The kids turn slowly, mouths open. Bhaap zips in loops above them, sparking with delight.

> **REHAN**
> This is... the weather?

> **MEGHNATH**
> The weather of Delhi. Somebody has to keep it.

> **TARA**
> Who?

Meghnath taps his own chest once. Then he picks up a broom.

> **MEGHNATH**
> Start sweeping.

---

## 4. APPRENTICESHIP — 07:00–10:00

(**BHAAP'S THEME** grows into a bouncy, brass-band-flavoured montage)

**EXT. ROOF — DAWN**
The kids, yawning, catch the dawn breeze with kites. Tara's kite rises steadily, exactly as she planned. Rehan's yanks, dives and drags him across the roof on his tummy. Ishaan's just floats. Meghnath unscrews a jar, the kites dip and let the breeze slide off into it, and he screws the lid on. A breeze in a bottle.

**EXT. ROOF — MIDDAY**
A thick grey smog blanket sags over the city. The kids sweep it up with enormous brooms, like gathering cobwebs, and stuff it into gunny sacks. Rehan sneezes a grey puff. Meghnath ties each sack with a neat knot.

**INT. TANK WORKSHOP — EVENING**
Tara copies everything into her notebook: arrows, diagrams, a drawing of a cloud with "warm + slow" next to it. Meghnath peers over her shoulder, grunts. Is it approval? Tara decides it is.

**EXT. ROOF / LOOM — EVENING**
Ishaan and Bhaap are inseparable. Bhaap sits on his head like a hat and keeps him cool. When Ishaan laughs, Bhaap sparks. When Ishaan stubs his toe, Bhaap drizzles on it.

**EXT. ROOF / LOOM — DAY** (comedy failure)
Rehan's turn to spin a cloud.

> **MEGHNATH**
> Slowly. Slowly. You are spinning a cloud, not bowling a bouncer.

Rehan nods, then spins as fast as he can. The cloud goes dark, hard and lumpy, rattling like a box of marbles.

> **MEGHNATH**
> Oh no.

The little black cloud shoots off the roof.

**EXT. COLONY CRICKET GROUND — CONTINUOUS**
From above: the colony kids' cricket match (small distant figures, a dusty pitch). The black cloud stops directly over the stumps and **hails**: a mini hailstorm the size of a bedsheet. Tiny ice balls bounce off a helmet. The stumps fall over. The batsman (a distant small figure) runs in circles. Off-screen voices howl.

**EXT. ROOF — CONTINUOUS**
Rehan stares. Meghnath slowly lowers his face into his good hand. Tara writes: "Don't spin fast." Underlines it three times.

> **ISHAAN**
> Captain says that was the best day of his life.

**INT. TANK WORKSHOP — NIGHT**
The kids leave, tired and happy. Meghnath, alone, looks at a small framed photograph on a shelf: VARSHA, laughing, her hair full of wind. He wipes the glass with his cardigan cuff. Puts it back. Gently.

---

## 5. THE MIDPOINT — 10:00–12:00

**INT. TANK WORKSHOP — DAY**

A calendar on the wall is just pictures of skies, one per day. The last thirty are pictures of the sun. There is a little cloud sticker that should have been weeks ago, and it is long past.

> **TARA**
> The monsoon's late. It's very late.

Meghnath doesn't answer. He goes to the big shape under the dusty cloth in the middle of the workshop and pulls the cloth away.

The **MONSOON TRUNK**: a huge iron trunk, black-green, riveted, carved with clouds and peacocks. On its lid are **three keyholes**, set in a wide triangle, too far apart for one person to reach.

He takes three iron keys from a hook. Tries to stretch from one keyhole to another. Can't. He sits down heavily on the trunk.

> **MEGHNATH**
> It needs three.

He looks at the photograph on the shelf.

> **MEGHNATH**
> It always needed three.

Ishaan looks from the photo to the old man. Bhaap drifts down and settles quietly on Meghnath's shoulder.

**MEMORY — EXT. ROOF — MONSOON NIGHT (SILHOUETTE)** (**MONSOON THEME**, only a hint: four notes)

Two silhouettes against a bruised purple sky, a man and a woman, laughing. Each turns a key on the trunk. Between them, a tiny cloud (Bhaap) sits on the third key and turns it with his whole body. The lid lifts and light spills out...

**BACK TO SCENE**

> **MEGHNATH**
> I'm done. I'm old. The city can manage on its own now.

He hangs the keys back on the hook.

> **TARA**
> But it can't! It's too hot. People are—

> **MEGHNATH**
> GO HOME.

It isn't cruel. It's tired. The kids go. Bhaap stays on his shoulder, and drizzles a little.

---

## 6. THE SHORTCUT — 12:00–15:00

**INT. TARA'S FLAT — NIGHT**

Dadi breathes slowly on her bed under the dead fan. Tara sits beside her, fanning her with the notebook. Tara's face sets.

**EXT. ROOF — NIGHT**

Tara and Rehan sneak back through the gap in the door.

> **REHAN**
> We just push the clouds in. Push them. Fast.

> **TARA**
> We need wind. A lot of wind.

**INT. TANK WORKSHOP — NIGHT**

Torch beams. They pass the jars of gentle breezes. Rehan stops at the sealed **tin trunk** in the corner with its chains, and a drawing on it of a swirling brown cloud.

> **TARA** *(reading her notes)*
> Uncle said never—

> **REHAN**
> He's not here.

They unwind the chains. Rehan lifts the lid one crack.

A **ROAR**.

**EXT. DELHI — NIGHT INTO DAY**

A **DUST STORM**, an aandhi, explodes out of the roof, and the whole world turns brown-orange monochrome. A wall of dust rolls over the city like a wave. Laundry rips off a hundred balconies and flaps away like startled birds. Pigeons tumble. Shop shutters rattle. A plastic chair cartwheels down the street.

**EXT. ROOF — CONTINUOUS**

Tara and Rehan wrestle the lid. Ishaan runs onto the roof, screaming for Bhaap. The little cloud is tumbling in the wind and grabs for Ishaan's fingers... misses... and is **blown away** into the brown sky, getting smaller and smaller.

> **ISHAAN**
> BHAAP!

Meghnath bursts out of the stairwell door in his cardigan, sling and all. With one arm he heaves the tin trunk's lid down. *KLANG.* Then he staggers to the antenna loom and begins to spin the storm backwards, a huge spiral, his whole body in it, pulling the dust back down out of the sky like a fisherman hauling a net...

The brown drains away. Colour returns.

Meghnath sways. Sits down hard against a water tank. His eyes close.

> **TARA**
> Uncle?

He waves his good hand weakly without opening his eyes.

> **MEGHNATH**
> Just... tired. Very... tired.

He breathes. He's asleep, sitting up.

**EXT. BLOCK C COURTYARD — DAY**

Neighbours (off-screen voices, balconies, laundry hanging in rags) argue.

> **VOICE (O.S.)**
> Those kids were on the roof!

> **ANOTHER VOICE (O.S.)**
> I SAW them!

Tara and Rehan stand at the bottom of the stairs, small, while the voices rain down. Ishaan sits on the top step, staring at an empty sky.

---

## 7. CLIMAX — 15:00–18:00

**INT. MEGHNATH'S FLAT — NIGHT**

Meghnath lies on his bed under a blanket, eyes open, defeated. The photograph of Varsha is on his bedside table, face down. Tara quietly sets it upright and tiptoes out.

**EXT. ROOF — NIGHT**

Ishaan sits alone on the parapet, legs swinging, looking at the sky. He's brought a pressure cooker lid. He waits.

He waits more. (**BHAAP'S THEME**, slow and small)

Stars. A plane blinking across. Captain the pigeon lands beside him and waits too.

Far off, something small and grey drifts toward them. It's the size of a fist, its dot eyes tired.

> **ISHAAN** *(whisper)*
> Bhaap.

The tiny grey cloud lands in Ishaan's hands, shivering, drizzling. Ishaan hugs it. Slowly it whitens and fluffs. Tzik: one small, happy spark.

Tara and Rehan stand behind him. They've been there a while.

> **REHAN**
> I'm sorry. About the trunk.

> **TARA**
> I'm sorry about my plan.

> **ISHAAN**
> Bhaap says the trunk needs three.

They look at each other. Three of them.

**INT. TANK WORKSHOP — NIGHT**

The three kids and Bhaap stand around the Monsoon Trunk. Three keys from the hook. Tara takes out her notebook, tears out a page and draws a careful plan: who stands where, arrows, a cloud.

> **TARA**
> We don't ask for anything. Not ice cream, not thank you. Nothing.

> **REHAN**
> Nothing.

> **ISHAAN**
> Nothing.

Bhaap sparks in agreement.

**EXT. ROOF — NIGHT** (**MONSOON THEME** begins to build)

They drag the trunk out under the open sky. Tara at the first keyhole. Ishaan, with Bhaap, at the second. Rehan at the third, but as he steps forward, the night wind gusts, the third key is torn from his fingers, and it skitters across the roof toward the edge.

Rehan dives. Gets it. But the gusts are rising, a wind trying to push him back.

> **TARA**
> You can't walk it over. Throw it!

> **REHAN**
> It's a key, not a ball!

> **TARA**
> Then bowl it!

Rehan looks down the length of the roof. The keyhole is small and far away. He takes his long run-up, past the tanks and under the antennas, the wind in his face, and bowls the key like the last ball of a final...

**SLOW MOTION**: the key spins end over end across the roof, through the wind...

...and **clicks** straight into the third keyhole.

> **TARA**
> Now. All together.

Tara turns hers. Rehan grabs his. Ishaan and Bhaap turn the third together, Bhaap pushing with his whole fluffy body.

*CLACK. CLACK. CLACK.*

The lid of the Monsoon Trunk lifts.

**EXT. DELHI — NIGHT** (**MONSOON THEME**, full)

Out of the trunk rolls the **monsoon**: a soft mountain of deep slate-blue cloud that keeps coming and coming, rolling over the parapet, over Block C, over the colony, over the whole skyline of Delhi, in one great sweep. Lightning stitches its edges. The heat haze shivers and breaks.

Silence.

Then, on the tin roof of the stair hut, **one fat raindrop**. *Tunk.*

Another. *Tunk.* *Tunk-tunk.*

---

## 8. THE FIRST RAIN — 18:00–19:30

**EXT. BLOCK C — NIGHT INTO DAWN**

The rain arrives all at once, a roar of it. The colours of the film flood in: saturated greens, deep slate blues, everything glistening and wet.

On every balcony of Block C, neighbours (feet, hands, silhouettes, dripping laundry) lean out, faces up. Off-screen whoops and cheers. Someone bangs a steel plate like a drum. Distant figures of cricket kids slide through the mud.

**EXT. ROOF — CONTINUOUS**

Tara, Rehan and Ishaan dance in the rain. Bhaap spins above them, sparking for joy. Captain the pigeon takes a bath in a puddle.

**INT. MEGHNATH'S FLAT — CONTINUOUS**

Rain on the window. Meghnath's eyes open. The curtain blows in, and a single drop lands on his face.

He gets up. Opens the window wide. Leans out into it. The rain soaks his eyebrows flat. And he **laughs**, a big, creaky, surprised laugh, the first one in the film.

**EXT. ROOF — DAWN**

Meghnath comes up the stairs. The kids stop dancing. Rehan holds out the third key, sheepishly.

Meghnath looks at the open trunk, at the three of them, at the little cloud. He reaches into his cardigan pocket and pulls out a battered, water-stained notebook bound with string: his weather notebook. He holds it out to Tara.

> **MEGHNATH**
> It needs three.

Tara takes it with both hands.

Meghnath sits down on his upturned bucket in the rain, folds his good hand over his sling, tips his face up, and, for the first time, **rests**.

---

## 9. CODA — 19:30–20:00

**INT. TANK WORKSHOP — WEEKS LATER**

The notebook lies open. The new pages are in Tara's handwriting: neat, with arrows, and a drawing of a cloud with "warm + slow" beside it.

**EXT. SCHOOL ROAD — DAY**

A hot, bright day. Ishaan walks to school with his bag, under a tiny personal rain cloud: Bhaap, raining very gently on him alone. Other kids (small, distant) stare. Ishaan waves.

**EXT. BLOCK C — DAY**

On the top-floor balcony, Meghnath in a short-sleeved shirt (no cardigan, no sling) waters a pot of new green chillies. Below, Rehan and Tara wave up at him.

> **REHAN**
> Morning, Meghnath Uncle!

Meghnath scowls. Then he waves back.

**THE END**
